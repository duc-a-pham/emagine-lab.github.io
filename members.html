EMAGINE Laboratory Members

We are actively recruiting highly motivated Ph.D., M.S., and undergraduate students, as well as visiting scholars and research collaborators. If you are passionate about applied electromagnetics, wireless technologies (i.e., antenna, RF sensors, and other EM devices), and hardware cybersecurity, we invite you to contact Prof. Pham at ducpham.mmw@gmail.com and become part of our growing research team to establish the future of intelligent and secured electromagnetic systems.
